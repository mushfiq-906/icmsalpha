CONCLUSION:
   File co-location is the DOMINANT driver (r=0.802).
   A gcid is independent when its clone peers are in different files.
   A gcid is dependent when it shares a file with its clone peers.
   Author identity is significant (F=17.3) but secondary.
   The same author produces both independent and dependent gcids
   depending on whether their gcids have same-file clone peers.
